## Stack

Existing static single-page site: `index.html` (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS) with GSAP + ScrollTrigger and Iconify icons, served from project root with local `images/` assets. The empty Nuxt starter scaffold under `InstitutoAG/` is leftover and out of scope.

## Users

Primary visitor: a person in pain — typically with an orthopedic injury or condition (back, shoulder, knee, hip, post-operative) — looking to relieve pain, restore movement, and book physiotherapy. Audience also includes elderly people and home-office workers seeking posture care or at-home treatment.

## Product Purpose

Instituto AG is the orthopedic physiotherapy clinic of Dra. Andressa Gomes. The site converts visitors into booked patients by establishing clinical credibility and making it easy to reach the clinic via WhatsApp. Success is a visitor scheduling an evaluation.

## Positioning

Personalized and humanized care: treatments tailored to each body, combining technique with empathy. Differentiation rests on a treatment style that respects the patient's body, understands their pain, and works with them toward full recovery — across clinical, at-home, and online settings.
